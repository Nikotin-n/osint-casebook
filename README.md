# 🔍 OSINT & Reconnaissance Investigations

Welcome! This repository contains writeups, methodologies, and practical cases focused on Open Source Intelligence (**OSINT**) and Geospatial Intelligence (**GEOINT**). 

The primary goal of this repository is to document analytical workflows, demonstrate data-gathering techniques, and showcase automation in open-source investigations.

---

## 🎯 Key Investigation Domains

* **GEOINT & IMINT (Geospatial & Image Intelligence):**
  * Pinpointing exact locations using subtle background details, architecture, flora, shadows, and sun angles (SunCalc).
  * Extracting and analyzing file metadata (EXIF data and extended attributes).
  * Satellite imagery analysis (Google Earth Pro, Sentinel Hub, Planet Labs).

* **SOCMINT & Person Investigation (Social Media Intelligence):**
  * Cross-referencing accounts, email addresses, phone numbers, and usernames across multiple platforms.
  * Breach data analysis, footprint mapping, and digital identity tracking.
  * Analyzing activity across Telegram, forums, messaging apps, and social networks.

* **Corporate & Domain Reconnaissance:**
  * Passive infrastructure discovery (Shodan, Censys, VirusTotal, SecurityTrails).
  * Domain research using WHOIS, DNS records, SSL/TLS certificates, and subdomains.
  * Uncovering deleted or historical content via web archives (Wayback Machine, Google Cache, archive.today).

## 💡 Analytical Workflow & Methodology

Every case or challenge in this repository follows a structured, analytical approach:

1. **Initial Assessment & Input:** Analyzing provided photos, usernames, domains, or data fragments.
2. **Hypothesis Generation:** Identifying landmarks, unique features, or digital footprints to form initial leads.
3. **Step-by-Step Execution:** Documenting tool selection, search operators, and analytical reasoning for each step.
4. **Rabbit Holes & Pivot Points:** Highlighting discarded leads and missteps to demonstrate critical thinking and adaptability.
5. **Final Findings & Verification:** Confirming precise coordinates, verified profiles, or mapping entity relationships.
