## 🔍 GEOINT Methodology & Workflow

### 1. Initial Visual Inspection & Regional Identification
* **Environmental & Terrain Analysis:**
  * Background analysis revealed a prominent snow-capped mountain range in the distance[cite: 1].
  * The unique mountain silhouette aligns with Mount Ararat, a key geographic landmark visible from the Ararat Plain.
* **Architectural Style & City Layout:**
  * Examination of residential structure, roof styles, building density, and street layout pointed toward urban residential areas in Armenia, specifically the Kentron district of Yerevan[cite: 1].

### 2. Feature Extraction & Landmark Identification
* **Primary Key Landmark (Lower Left):**
  * Identified the distinct circular, illuminated structure on the lower-left side: **Alexander Spendiaryan Opera and Ballet National Theatre**[cite: 1].
* **Urban Infrastructure:**
  * Analyzed the adjacent road patterns, lit boulevards, and grid-like green zones/squares along the boulevard leading to the Opera House[cite: 1].
* **Perspective & Elevation Analysis:**
  * The wide-angle, downward perspective indicates the photograph was taken from an elevated observation platform located north of the city center, facing south towards Mount Ararat.

### 3. Triangulation & Geolocation Verification
* By mapping the line of sight (LOS) from the elevated viewpoint through the Opera Theatre towards Mount Ararat, the exact camera location was pinpointed to the upper tiers/viewpoint of the **Yerevan Cascade complex**.
* **Confirmed Coordinates:**
  * **Latitude:** `40.19120`
  * **Longitude:** `44.51550`
